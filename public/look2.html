Why this reads better

Pine & Powder is just as dark as GroundLink is today — so darkness was never the problem. What it has is bigger text, white ink instead of grey, and labels that are tracked and coloured instead of whispered. The daylight mode is the other half: your own Field rule has always asked for one.
